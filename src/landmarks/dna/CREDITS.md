# DNA landmark credits

- `public/assets/dna/1BNA.pdb` — PDB entry 1BNA, "Structure of a B-DNA dodecamer. Conformation and dynamics"
  (Drew, Wing, Takano, Broka, Tanaka, Itakura, Dickerson, 1981). Source: https://files.rcsb.org/download/1BNA.pdb
  Licence: Protein Data Bank archive data, CC0 1.0 (public domain).
- Method: only ATOM records are read (water/HETATM ignored). The 12-bp structure is re-registered onto an exact
  10.5 bp / 3.4 nm screw and tiled along a curved axis; missing 5' phosphates at the block seams are rebuilt geometrically.
